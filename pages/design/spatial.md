# Spatial Layout Rules

Liquid Glass design shifts the interface toward spatial floating containers. Navigation panels, menus, and viewport layers sit in a suspended arrangement.

### Key Rules
1. **Independent Modules:** Elements no longer span edge-to-edge; they float above the background.
2. **Gaps & Margins:** Soft, structured offsets prevent overlapping layers from feeling cluttered.
3. **Refractive Boundaries:** Double-rim highlights mimic the way physical light bends along glass edges.
