# Warping Logic

A major aspect of modern glass materials is the distortion of light passing through the boundary surfaces.

### Simulated Depth
Since actual, real-time backdrop refraction has limited browser support, we simulate this physical look by styling borders with high-contrast bevel gradients. 

Combined with deep background blurs, these specular borders emulate a realistic "lensing" curvature, focusing light along the outer boundaries of the container.
